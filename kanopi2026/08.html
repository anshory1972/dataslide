The reform path
Pay poor households directly and accurately first. Then move subsidies from products to people.
1
DPI in placeDigital ID, data exchange (SPLP) and digital payments.
2
Know who needs helpOne dynamic registry; fewer exclusion and inclusion errors.
3
Integrate the programmesPKH, Sembako and PIP as one family benefit, one rule, one payment.
4
Compensate directlyCash by name to eligible households when energy prices change.
5
Reform energy subsidiesLPG 3 kg, fuel and electricity move towards cost; the poor are protected. Kerosene to LPG (2007) shows it can be done.
Refocus and integrate. Move price subsidies to named households, on one registry and one payment channel. This is how social protection becomes inclusive and fiscally sustainable.
