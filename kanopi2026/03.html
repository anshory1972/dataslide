Indonesia spends little on social assistance, but not for lack of money

Most of the money goes to energy subsidies instead.
Social assistance spending, % of GDP Cash transfers, in-kind aid, social pensions, public works and fee waivers.
Who gets the energy subsidies Share of all energy subsidy benefits, by consumption decile, %. The richest 10% get 15.9%, the poorest 10% get 6.7%.
24.6%of fuel (BBM) compensation goes to the richest 10% The poorest 10% get 3.4%.
The money is there Moving energy subsidies to named poor households would bring Indonesia to the level of its peers.
Source: World Bank, 360 Targeting Review of Social Assistance (forthcoming), ASPIRE 2024: social assistance shares, energy subsidies and compensation 1.7–2.0% of GDP, and benefit shares by decile (Susenas March 2023, all energy subsidies). BBM compensation: DEN staff calculation, Susenas March 2025. Compiled in Yusuf, “Satu Dana Perlinsos”, BP Taskin, 14 October 2026.
