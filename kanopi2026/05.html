Why targeting misses, and how DPI can help

Three causes of error. Digital public infrastructure (DPI) acts on two of them.
Why targeting is inaccurate
Exclusion and inclusion errors have three main causes.
1

The quota is smaller than the eligible group BUDGET

The number of places is set before eligibility. PKH: about 20 m households eligible, 10 m places. Sembako: 26 m eligible, 18.3 m places. Households left out count as exclusion error.

2

The process is slow and leaks DPI

Nomination through village meetings, verification by many offices, payment problems, hard-to-reach areas, leakage and elite capture of who gets listed.

3

The formula puts households in the wrong decile DPI

Welfare y is not observed. A proxy means test (PMT) predicts it from household characteristics x:

y = f(x) + e

x is old (Regsosek 2022) and often inaccurate. The error e moves households across the cut-off:

70%misplaced among 10 deciles
50%among 5 groups
20%between 2 groups
Cause 1 needs a budget decision, not technology: the number of places should follow eligibility.
How DPI helps, from registration to payment
One record and one process for every programme.
  • 1
    Registeron demand, alone or with an agent
    Households apply when their situation changes, not only at a census.
  • 2
    Verifythrough SPLP data exchange
    Answers checked against administrative data.
    DukcapilPLNBPJSATR/BPNKorlantasSLIK/OJK
  • 3
    UpdateDTSEN becomes dynamic
    The decile is recomputed from current data, so x is no longer old.
  • 4
    Rankshort formula, 8–9 variables
    Variables that can be verified, so fewer households are misclassified.
  • 5
    Decide and appealone eligibility record
    A complaint corrects the record for every programme.
  • 6
    PayG2P, to an account by NIK
    Payment reaches the person named, and every transfer can be traced.
