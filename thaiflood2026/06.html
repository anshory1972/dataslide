ThaiTERM: how a flood reaches other provinces

A bottom-up multi-regional CGE model of the TERM type (Horridge, Madden and Wittwer 2005)
B · Model and shocks
Each of the 77 provinces has its own industries, prices, households and input-output structure. Buyers switch between provinces: elasticity 5 for goods, 1 for services.
Source: Mungsunti, Kewprasopsak and Yusuf (2026), Economy-wide losses from river-basin floods in Thailand, ThaiTERM results. Section 3.1; Appendix B. Koks et al. (2016, pp. 1919-1920) on losses and gains outside the hit area.
