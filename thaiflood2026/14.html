Outside the basin: losses and gains side by side

Only a model with all 77 provinces can show both from the same flood
C · Results
Most provinces outside the basin have no contraction; farm provinces gain as buyers replace lost farm output. The gains depend on how easily buyers switch.
Source: Mungsunti, Kewprasopsak and Yusuf (2026), Economy-wide losses from river-basin floods in Thailand, ThaiTERM results. Section 4.3. In 95 of the 98 results above +0.01%, agriculture contributes most. Construction also rises in some provinces: an effect of the closure (fixed national investment).
