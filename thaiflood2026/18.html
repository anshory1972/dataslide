Limits and further work

ThaiTERM is not specific to floods; it can be used for other regional questions in Thailand
D · Policy and limits
output loss on all inputs; fixed real wages; capital out of use for the rest of the year
not in the flood maps; the manufacturing loss in the central basins is an upper value
substitution between provinces is easier than between specific products
floods of linked basins, such as the whole Chao Phraya system, are not studied
canals can make flooding worse where the water goes (Padiyedath Gopalan et al. 2022, p. 16)
five to eight sectors, placed with factory locations
the whole Chao Phraya system with its upper basins
from factory and urban land to paddy, as canals and retention areas do
occupations from survey data; a dynamic version with rebuilding
The limits mostly raise the level of the loss in every basin. The comparison of basins holds in the four sensitivity tests.
Source: Mungsunti, Kewprasopsak and Yusuf (2026), Economy-wide losses from river-basin floods in Thailand, ThaiTERM results. Section 4.7; Section 5.
