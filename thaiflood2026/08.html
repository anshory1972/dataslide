Flood shocks: where they enter production

From 16 years of flood maps to the production function of each sector in each province, in levels
B · Model and shocks
Q = (1 / ATOT) · F(K, L, N, Z)
Q output; K capital; L labour; N land; Z intermediate inputs
s = flooded land of a sector / all its land in the province
Output lost: ATOT
atot = 100 [1/(1 − ℓ) − 1]
ℓ = s · 21/365; crops s · 0.37
Capital destroyed: K
xcap = −100 s δ · 344/365
δ = 0.248 or 0.0132 by sector
The flood enters production twice: a higher ATOT lowers output Q from the same inputs, and the capital stock K falls.
Source: Mungsunti, Kewprasopsak and Yusuf (2026), Economy-wide losses from river-basin floods in Thailand, ThaiTERM results. Section 3.3; Appendix B, B1-B2; Appendix C. atot and xcap are the % changes of ATOT and K. Drawing is schematic.
