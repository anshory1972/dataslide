Randomised experiment: does a reminder raise saving?

Two random draws. The first tests the effect and the second tests the reason.
2 · Why credibility
1 · The question
People want to save and do not. Is it lack of money, or lack of attention?
If attention is the cause, a message that costs almost nothing can raise saving as a subsidy does.
3 banks
Bolivia, Peru, the Philippines
2 · The design
Chance decided who was reminded, then which words were used.
3 · The result
A reminder raised saving. It worked when it named the saver’s own goal.
