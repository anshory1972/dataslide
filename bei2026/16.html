1 · The question
Does a cap on fees protect consumers, or do banks take it back elsewhere?
Regulators limit fees. Banks can answer with higher interest or less credit. Only a comparison group can show which happened.

credit card accounts, month by month, 2008 to 2012
2 · The design
The law covered one kind of card and left a similar kind out.
3 · The result
The cap lowered what consumers paid. Banks did not shift the cost.
Source: Agarwal, Chomsisengphet, Mahoney and Stroebel (2015), “Regulating Consumer Financial Products: Evidence from Credit Cards”, Quarterly Journal of Economics 130(1): 111–164, abstract and introduction. The lines are a drawing, not the data.
