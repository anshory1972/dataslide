Four rules for using AI in research

None of them needs new software.
4 · Integrity
Open every reference
If you have not read it, do not cite it.
Test after the training date
A model may already know the past.
Check a sample by hand
If a model labels your data, label 125–250 items yourself and compare.
The author is responsible
State where AI was used. The author answers for every line.
Sources: Buchanan, Hill and Shapoval (2024); Lopez-Lira and Tang (2026); Ludwig, Mullainathan and Rambachan (2025), NBER Working Paper 33344, pp. 16, 32–33; Korinek (2023, 2024).
