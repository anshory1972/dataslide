Randomised experiment: do investors care about impact?

A designed choice with real investors. No bank or government partner was needed.
2 · Why credibility
1 · The question
Do investors pay for impact, or for the feeling of doing good?
Sustainable finance assumes investors reward funds that do more good. If they do not, funds have no reason to deliver more.
10×
more impact shown to one group than to the other
2 · The design
Chance decided which version each investor saw, next to an ordinary fund.
3 · The result
Investors pay for a sustainable fund. They do not pay more for more impact.
Source: Heeb, Kölbel, Paetzold and Zeisberger (2023), “Do Investors Care about Impact?”, Review of Financial Studies 36(5): 1737–1787, Figure 3 and pp. 1738–1739. Preregistered; 527 private investors in the panel, the Netherlands.
