1991, schooling: a birth date decides who may leave school early

Joshua Angrist and Alan Krueger. Nobel Prize 2021.
2 · Why credibility
1 · The question
How much more do you earn for one more year of school?
Educated people earn more. But able people study longer and would earn more anyway. Ability is not in the data.
7%
more earnings for each year of school, in a simple comparison
2 · The design
A birth date and a school-leaving law create the comparison.
3 · The result
Schooling itself raises earnings, by about 9% a year.
Source: Nobel Committee (2021), Scientific Background, pp. 5–13, and Popular Science Background, pp. 2–3 and 6–7, describing Angrist and Krueger (1991). US men born 1930–39, 1980 census. The original paper was not consulted.
