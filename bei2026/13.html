Randomised experiment: knowledge or price?

Indonesia. Published in the Journal of Finance.
2 · Why credibility
1 · The question
Do people stay without a bank account for lack of knowledge, or because of the price?
Two policies compete: teach financial literacy, or make an account cheaper. Governments pay for both.
60%
of the households surveyed had no bank account
2 · The design
Each household got a reward size and a class invitation, both by chance.
3 · The result
In surveys, literacy and accounts are correlated. In the experiment, only the price changed behaviour.
Source: Cole, Sampson and Zia (2011), “Prices or Knowledge? What Drives Demand for Financial Services in Emerging Markets?”, Journal of Finance 66(6): 1933–1967, Table IV: 736 of 1,230 surveyed households had no account; 564 took part.
