1994, wages: two states, one raises the minimum wage

David Card and Alan Krueger. Nobel Prize 2021.
2 · Why credibility
1 · The question
Does a higher minimum wage destroy jobs?
Textbooks said yes. But in bad times jobs fall and governments also raise the minimum wage. The two move together without one causing the other.
$5.05
New Jersey's new minimum wage per hour, up from $4.25
2 · The design
One state changed the rule. Its neighbour did not.
3 · The result
The fall in jobs that textbooks predicted did not occur.
