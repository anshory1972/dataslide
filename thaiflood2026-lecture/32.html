The economy without the shock. Results are % differences from it.
A change we put into the model: here, output lost and capital destroyed by a flood.
The choice of fixed variables (exogenous). The model solves for the others (endogenous).
Supply equals demand in every market, at the prices the model finds.
All markets solved together, so prices and incomes affect each other.
Value added at constant prices; here for each province and for Thailand.
How easily buyers switch between sources: 5 for goods, 1 for services, between provinces.
About one year: capital and land fixed; real wage fixed, so employment adjusts.
Each province has its own economy; national results add up the provinces.
Flooded land of a sector divided by all land of the sector in the province.
These ten terms are enough to read the results of a CGE study.
CGE: computable general equilibrium.
