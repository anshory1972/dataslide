A small example: two provinces, one flood

Follow the numbered steps
B · CGE from the beginning
1  Rice output falls: fields under water.
2  The rice price rises.
5  Household income falls; they buy less, also from B.
3  Buyers switch to B's rice. How many switch depends on the substitution elasticity.
5  A buys less from B.
4  Farms sell more; farm jobs and land rents rise.
6  Net result for B: the gain from 3 and 4 minus the loss from 5. It can be + or −.
The same flood causes losses in A and can cause gains in B. ThaiTERM follows these steps for 18 sectors in 77 provinces at the same time.
