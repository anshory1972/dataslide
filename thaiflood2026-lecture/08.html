One market: supply, demand and price

What a flood does to the market for rice
B · CGE from the beginning
The flood destroys crops. At each price, farmers can sell less: the supply line moves left.
At the old price, buyers want more rice than is for sale. The price rises.
A new equilibrium: a higher price (P1) and a smaller quantity (Q1). Some buyers buy rice from elsewhere.
Equilibrium: the price at which the quantity supplied equals the quantity demanded.
