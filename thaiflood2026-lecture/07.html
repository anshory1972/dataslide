A model economy: the circular flow

Who buys from whom, and who pays whom
B · CGE from the beginning
Each flow is an equation, with numbers from the database.
Each market clears: supply equals demand.
Each account balances: costs equal sales; income equals spending plus saving.
After a shock, prices change until all markets clear again.
A CGE model is this circular flow, written as equations for each market and, in ThaiTERM, for each province.
