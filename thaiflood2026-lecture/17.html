From a flood map to a model shock

Four steps, repeated for each basin, province and sector
C · From a flood to a shock
16 years of maps (GISTDA, HII). For each basin: the mean flooded area per year in each of its provinces.
The land-use map shows which land is under water: paddy, other crops, factories, housing, roads.
For each sector: flooded land of the sector divided by all land of the sector in the province.
The share sets the output lost and the capital destroyed in each sector of each province.
Each sector is shocked only by the flooding of its own land in the province. The next slide shows the numbers for one province.
Source: Mungsunti, Kewprasopsak and Yusuf (2026), Economy-wide losses from river-basin floods in Thailand, ThaiTERM results. Section 3.3. Drawings are schematic.
