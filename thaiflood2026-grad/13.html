Elasticities: values and sources

The behavioural parameters of ThaiTERM
B · How ThaiTERM works

σP and σDI: GTAP 12 values for Thailand, weighted averages over the GTAP commodities of each sector.

ε: twice σDI.

σDD: standard TERM values; tested at one third in the sensitivity analysis.

Other values: occupations σL = 0.35; margin origins σM = 0.2; Frisch parameter −2.7, from a formula in income per head.

Low σP in agriculture and mining: land and capital are hard to replace with labour.

The elasticity between provinces, σDD, decides how much output moves to other provinces after a flood.
Source: Mungsunti, Kewprasopsak and Yusuf (2026), Economy-wide losses from river-basin floods in Thailand, ThaiTERM results. Appendix D, Table D1. The eight other services all have the values shown.
