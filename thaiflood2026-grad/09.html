The demand side of trade between provinces
B · How ThaiTERM works
B5
xc,s,u,d = xc,u,d − σDIc (ppurc,s,u,d − ppurc,u,d)
each user chooses domestic or imported; σDI from GTAP 12, 1.9 to 7.6
B6
xtradc,s,r,d = xusec,s,d − σDDc (pdelc,s,r,d − pusec,s,d)
all users of d together buy from all origins r, by delivered price
σDD is 5 for agriculture, mining and manufacturing, 1 for most services and 0.5 for real estate. A low value means buyers keep their usual sources when prices change. This is the bottom-up part of TERM: one sourcing decision per commodity and province of use.
Two nests: first domestic or imported, then the province of origin. Buyers switch origin when delivered prices change.
Source: Mungsunti, Kewprasopsak and Yusuf (2026), Economy-wide losses from river-basin floods in Thailand, ThaiTERM results. Section 3.1; Appendix B, equations B5-B6; Appendix D. Line widths are schematic.
