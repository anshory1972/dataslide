Why some provinces gain

Small gains in farm provinces far from the flooded basin
E · Explaining the results
All 22 runs: 98 results outside the flooded basin are above +0.01%; in 95 of them agriculture contributes most. With elasticities one third as large, 20 results are above +0.01%.
Gains come from substitution: buyers replace lost output with output from other provinces.
Source: Mungsunti, Kewprasopsak and Yusuf (2026), Economy-wide losses from river-basin floods in Thailand, ThaiTERM results. Section 4; Section 4.5 for the test with lower elasticities.
