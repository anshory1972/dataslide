B9
xsuppmarm,r,d,p = xmarm,r,d − σM (pdomm,p − pmarm,r,d)
margin supplied by province p
In the database, margins come from the NESDC table; their use on each route rises with the square root of road distance.
A buyer in d pays the basic price in r plus margins that depend on the route. A flood in r raises the delivered price everywhere r sells.
