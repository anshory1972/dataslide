WHY IT MATTERS
Four services, two ways to build
Separate systems: 12 systems, records that do not match
DPI: 3 shared blocks, one record per person
POTENTIAL BENEFITS
  • Government: faster, cheaper services
  • Firms: more competition and innovation
  • People: easier access, more control
RISKS TO MANAGE
  • Exclusion of people with low digital access or skills
  • Misuse of personal data
  • Lock-in, poor-quality systems; weak capacity
1 · FOUNDATIONAL
Essential, for everyone
Basic functions at the scale of the whole society, used across sectors and population groups. Can be built or run by the government, by firms, or both.
2 · BUILDING BLOCKS
Reused, not rebuilt
Interoperable and based on open standards and open APIs, so many services plug into the same block. Modular and minimal: no more central data than needed.
3 · PUBLIC BENEFIT
Safe and inclusive by design
Inclusion and user choice, privacy by design, and strong governance with public oversight. Built into the technical design, not added later.
Source: World Bank (2025), Digital Public Infrastructure and Development: A World Bank Group Approach, Digital Transformation White Paper Vol. 1: definition, characteristics and ecosystem pp. 7–10 (Figures 2–4); benefits and risks pp. 5, 9, 29 (Figures 1, 3, 13).
