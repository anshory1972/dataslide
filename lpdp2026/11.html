A FUNDING DECISION
Budget: fewer places than eligible households
The quota is set before eligibility. PKH: about 20 m households potentially eligible, 10 m places. Sembako: 26 m eligible, 18.3 m places. Those left out count as exclusion error.

DPI
Wrong deciles: the PMT formula misclassifies
Welfare y is not observed, so a proxy means test (PMT) predicts it from household characteristics x.
y = ŷ + e = f(x) + e
Households are ranked by ŷ alone. But x is old (Regsosek 2022) and inaccurate, and e, what x cannot explain, moves households across the cut-off.
