Why the climate agenda is likely to fail, and how to change the incentives

The benefits of cutting emissions are global and long term, but the costs are local and immediate. Each country is better off if others act, a prisoner's dilemma (Yusuf 2025, p. 4). To change the outcome, the payoffs must change.
