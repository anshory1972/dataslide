Closing: inclusive and green development needs a strong state

DPI improves fiscal efficiency
More revenue

Linked data on people, assets, jobs and electricity use helps the state see who can pay tax.

Better spending

Help goes to people, not products: fewer errors, faster payment, less leakage.

Why the state must be strong
Markets do not fix inequality or pollution

Both are market failures. Only a government with enough fiscal capacity can correct them. A fiscally weak state cannot protect its people or its environment.

DPI makes the state stronger, so that it can protect people and the environment at the same time.
