Reflection: why do we need to improve social protection?

Does low productivity create poverty, or does poverty create low productivity?
A modern state protects its people

Social protection that reaches the right households, quickly, is a basic task of the state.

Social protection is an investment in growth

People are less productive because they are poor, not only the other way round. The evidence is below.

Do not tax the poor with paperwork

Long forms and interviews use up the mental capacity of the poor (p. 980). Simple digital registration avoids this.

The gap is not explained by

  • FoodFarmers did not eat less before harvest: Rs2,663 vs Rs2,592 a month on food.

  • StressHeart rate and blood pressure were higher before harvest, but controlling for them does not change the result.

How large is the effect

Like losing a full night of sleep About 13 IQ points in the laboratory study (New Jersey shoppers who thought about a $1,500 car repair). For the farmers, three quarters of that size.
