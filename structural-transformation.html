Agriculture’s share of value added and income per head

Source & method
Countries: World Bank World Development Indicators, GDP per capita (constant 2015 US$, NY.GDP.PCAP.KD) and agriculture, forestry and fishing value added as a share of GDP (NV.AGR.TOTL.ZS); most recent year in which a country reports both, 2015–2024, 202 countries. Indonesia’s path: the same two series for 1983–2024, the full period in which the World Bank reports the current-price agriculture share for Indonesia. The dashed segment, 1960–1982, uses agriculture value added over GDP with both measured in constant local currency (NV.AGR.TOTL.KD / NY.GDP.MKTP.KD), which is available from 1960 and differs from the current-price measure by 0.8 percentage points on average over the 42 years in which both exist. Provinces: BPS, PDRB by 17 industry categories at current prices and GRDP per capita, 2024, from the BPS Web API. Provincial income per head is placed on the same axis by scaling each province’s current-rupiah GRDP per head by the ratio of Indonesia’s 2024 GDP per head in constant 2015 US dollars to its 2024 GDP per head in current rupiah, so provincial relativities are those BPS reports and the national level is the World Bank’s.

Manufacturing’s share of value added, year by year

Source & method
Provinces: BPS, PDRB by 17 industry categories at current prices, annual, from the BPS Web API. The provincial series exists only from 2010, the first year of the 2010-base regional accounts; there is no earlier provincial breakdown. The four provinces created in 2022–2023 — Papua Selatan, Papua Tengah, Papua Pegunungan and Papua Barat Daya — appear as single points in 2024. Indonesia: World Bank, manufacturing value added as a share of GDP (NV.IND.MANF.ZS), 1983–2024. The national line is not the arithmetic sum of the provincial ones: summing provincial PDRB gives a manufacturing share 2.6 points higher in 2010 and 4.3 points higher in 2024, because provincial accounts and national GDP differ in coverage and in the treatment of taxes on products.
