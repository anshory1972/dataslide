Four implications for flood protection

They follow from the comparison of basins, not from the level of the loss
D · Policy and limits
The same kind of flood causes very different losses. Protecting one rai prevents a much larger loss in the central basins than in the farm basins. Priorities set by flooded area alone miss this.
About 10% of the loss falls in provinces with no land in the flooded basin. This supports national funding, and basin flood plans that count the effects outside the basin.
The loss is concentrated in a few provinces and in manufacturing. In the Chao Phraya run, Ayutthaya and Bangkok have 62% of the national loss.
Firms in the eastern provinces lose through their links with the central plain. Business continuity plans matter there too.
The results compare losses between basins. They do not include the cost of protection, so they do not say which project to build.
Source: Mungsunti, Kewprasopsak and Yusuf (2026), Economy-wide losses from river-basin floods in Thailand, ThaiTERM results. Section 4.6. Basin committees prepare flood plans (Thepprasit et al. 2026, p. 3).
