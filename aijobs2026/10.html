Q2 · Productivity, pay, inequality
True or false: labour-saving technology raises long-run unemployment?
True.
Textbook: false. It ignores the new jobs that technology creates. GPT-4o and o1 both said true.
Generative AI is a statistical model (the M in LLM), so it always has an error term. It sounds equally confident when wrong, and beginners are least able to see it.
