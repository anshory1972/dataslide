Faster today, less learning tomorrow

What happened in the exam without ChatGPT?
Q2 · Productivity, pay, inequality
After 10–15 minutes of AI help, people later solved 57% alone, against 74%.
Coders learning a new library with AI scored 15% lower on the skill quiz.
Essays written with ChatGPT: 83% could not quote them minutes later (18 people).
Generative AI that gives answers raises today’s score and lowers tomorrow’s. A tutor version that gives hints removed most of the exam loss.
Sources: Bastani et al. (2025), PNAS, Results; Liu et al. (2026), p. 4; Shen and Tamkin (2026), Anthropic, p. 2, 11; Kosmyna et al. (2025), MIT Media Lab preprint, p. 31 (n = 18). Learning from LLM summaries also gave shallower knowledge than web search (Melumad et al. 2025, PNAS Nexus). Painting: illustrative image generated with Midjourney.
