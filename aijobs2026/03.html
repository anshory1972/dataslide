Where generative AI arrives: young Indonesians and jobs

The starting point, before ChatGPT
Q1 · Young people and jobs
Youth unemployment 17.5% in Aug 2026, up from 16.9%. Graduates: only group above 2022.
Before ChatGPT, formal jobs fell in the industrial provinces; the young lost them fastest.
1996, before AI: 20–30% of young urban graduates were unemployed.
Generative AI arrives in a labour market where young Indonesians already find fewer formal jobs. The question is whether it makes this worse.
Sources: BPS, BRS No. 113/11/Th. XXIX (5 Nov 2026), Tabel 3, p. 13–14; BPS formal workers by province 2015–2025, Sakernas August, and author’s calculation; Manning and Junankar (1998), BIES, p. 58. Photograph: illustrative image generated with Midjourney.
