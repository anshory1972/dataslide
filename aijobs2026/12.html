Who gains the money

Productivity is one question; who receives it is another
Q2 · Productivity, pay, inequality
Juniors’ pay: US starting pay 7.5% lower in exposed jobs; Brazil, age 16–21: 4.7% lower.
Indonesia starts unequal: earnings Gini 0.38 (2001) → 0.48 (2015).
Emerging Asia adopts AI one to two decades later; young wage earners gain later.
Women were 16 points less likely than men in the same job to use ChatGPT for work (Denmark).
Owners of capital are likely to gain more than workers, and Indonesia later. Skills that work with AI matter more than subsidies for using it.
Sources: Johnston and Makridis (2026), p. 14; Azar, Gine and Sanz-Espín (2026), p. 21; Aguirre and Meloni (2026), p. 17–18; Humlum and Vestergaard (2024), PNAS; Che, Xin and Yoshida (2026), IMF WP/26/166, p. 3, 28–29; Sen (2024), BIES, p. 179, citing Yusuf and Halim (2023). Balance: schematic.
