Before and after ChatGPT

What is different
Q1 · Young people and jobs
Before: computers and robots
Tasks taken: routine: assembly, record keeping
Most exposed: less-educated workers; 56% of Indonesian jobs “at high risk” (2016)
Age: young workers least exposed
After ChatGPT
Tasks taken: writing, coding, analysis, customer service
Most exposed: graduates, women, clerical workers
Age: young and junior workers affected first
Ten years ago, education was the protection. Now the most exposed jobs need a degree, and the young are affected first.
Sources: Chang and Huynh (2016), ILO, PDF p. 24, 32; World Bank (2025), Future Jobs, p. xxix; Webb (2020), PDF p. 4–5; Eloundou et al. (2024), Science, p. 1307. Drawings: schematic.
