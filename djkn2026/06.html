Definition of a CGE model

A computable general equilibrium model: equations from economic theory, solved with data
A · CGE basics
Definition
“Many equations that determines prices and quantities of commodities and inputs characterized by optimizing rational economic agents who interact in a competitive market economy such that all the markets are in equilibrium.”
A. A. Yusuf (2012)
In practice: a system of non-linear equations with n equations, n endogenous variables and many exogenous variables.
As many equations as unknowns, often tens of thousands. A computer solves them, for example with GEMPACK. The result for a policy is the difference between two solutions: with and without the policy.
Definition from A. A. Yusuf, Economic Models for Policy Making (training deck), pp. 47-52.
