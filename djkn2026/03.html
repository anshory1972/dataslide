GTAP-InVEST: land change becomes productivity shocks

A global CGE model (37 regions × 18 agro-ecological zones) linked to spatial ecosystem models
B · Valuation in CGE
Projects land demand to 2030 by region and agro-ecological zone
Allocates the land change to grid cells of about 300 m
Computes each ecosystem service on the 2030 map
Solves again with the service changes as shocks
