A map is a model tooUseful because it is smaller than the land it shows
Indiae Orientalis nova descriptio, J. Janssonius, 17th century (BnF).
In a short story by Borges, map makers draw a map of an empire as large as the empire itself. It matches every point, and the next generation finds it of no use.
A CGE model keeps markets, prices, incomes and the budget, and leaves out the rest.
