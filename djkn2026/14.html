Sea-level rise takes half of the land: kS = −50%
Labour stays the same. Corn uses land more intensively than meat: K1/L1 > K2/L2.
Land per worker falls in both industries; rent rises relative to the wage.
The frontier shrinks most on the corn side; corn becomes dearer (steeper price line).
