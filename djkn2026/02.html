Why model? To see the world without the policy

A model builds the counterfactual that data alone cannot show
A · CGE basics
Before and after is not the same as with and withoutStylised example: an outcome, such as fuel use or poverty, around a policy change
effect of the policy
Comparing before and after mixes the policy with everything else that changed. A model compares with and without.
Why we use models
To judge a policy fairly
Compare the world with the policy against the same world without it.
To separate the policy from other changes
Prices, incomes and weather also change. The model keeps them fixed.
To run an experiment we cannot run
Change one policy and keep everything else the same, as in a laboratory.
To answer fast
A randomised trial takes years. Government often needs an answer in weeks.
