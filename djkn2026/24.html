InVEST models

Production functions: how a change in the structure of an ecosystem changes the flow of a service across the map.
Integrated Valuation of Ecosystem Services and Tradeoffs
Maps of each service, in
• physical units, e.g. tonnes of carbon, or
• money, e.g. present value of that carbon
The three ecosystem models used in GTAP-InVEST (two from InVEST, one from FISH-MIP)
Pollination
Natural land is pollinator habitat. For each farm cell, the model takes the share of natural land within 2 km.
30% or more = full pollination (index 0 to 1).
Carbon and timber
Carbon stock of each land-cover class in four pools, mostly IPCC Tier 1 values, for about 125 carbon zones.
Marine fisheries
Taken from FISH-MIP (EcoOcean food-web model): total catch biomass on a 0.5° grid, 51 species groups.
To GTAP: Yield shock for crops that need pollinators
To GTAP: Forestry productivity shock, same % as carbon
To GTAP: Fisheries productivity shock
In GTAP-InVEST, InVEST gives physical changes on the map; GTAP turns them into economic effects.
Source: Johnson et al. (2021), The Economic Case for Nature, World Bank (grey), p. 15 and Appendix B, pp. 104-105. Coastal protection and water yield models were not used in that report (p. 104, note 1).
