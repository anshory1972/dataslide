Set A: the model finds the subsidy rate that cuts land-use emissions by 26%: 379 of 1,459 Mt CO2e a year. Paid by Indonesian taxpayers.
Set B: a further 15% cut (219 Mt), paid with money from abroad.
The shaded area is a permanent cut, even if the policy stops at T1. Cost per tonne = S / (a·k), with S the yearly subsidy.
Rectangle: budget cost. Triangle: welfare cost. The rest is a transfer from taxpayers to landowners.
