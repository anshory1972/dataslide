Each zone has its own land rent. Land moves between uses inside a zone, not between zones.
0.25 → 0.5 → 1.0: CET values from bottom to top. A higher value means land moves more easily. Forest land is the hardest to move.
Green: now. Orange: after land is protected. Rent rises fast as land in use nears the limit. Protecting land moves the limit left, so protection has a cost.
GTAP-AEZ is GTAP with land split by zone and by use, measured in value. It is the economic model inside GTAP-InVEST.
Sources: Hertel, Lee, Rose and Sohngen (2008), GTAP WP 44, pp. 5-9 (grey); Johnson et al. (2021), The Economic Case for Nature, World Bank, Box 2 and pp. 16-17 (grey); Michetti (2012), p. 17 (grey). CET = constant elasticity of transformation.
