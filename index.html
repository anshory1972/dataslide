Dataslide

Data pages on Indonesian development. Each one is a single self-contained page, built from primary sources, on social protection, growth, research, or economy-wide modelling.

Social protection and poverty

  • Data page

    Digitalising Social Protection

    How much of Indonesia's social assistance reaches the households it is meant for, why the gap exists, and what digitalisation can and cannot fix.

  • Data page

    Fiscal Incidence of Free School Meals

    What Indonesia's free school meals programme is worth to the households that receive it, and how it sits against the transfers alongside it.

  • Teaching

    Predicting Welfare

    A teaching case in applied microeconometrics. One real targeting model, 1.02 million households, and what it shows about prediction versus inference.

  • Data page

    The Quality of Indonesia's New Jobs

    Indonesia added 1.9 million jobs in the year to August 2025 and 1.7 million were part-time. Four measures of job quality, straight from the BPS Web API.

Growth and structural change

  • Data page

    The Growth We Want

    Why the old growth model stopped working for most people, and what a better one has to deliver. Charts built from primary sources.

  • Data page

    The Future of Regional Growth

    Where growth actually comes from, how far Indonesia's regions still have to travel, and what evidence from Aceh and Kepulauan Riau shows.

  • Data page

    Where Indonesia's Next Growth Will Come From

    Evidence from three IndoTERM computable general equilibrium studies on growth strategy, downstreaming and regional structural transformation.

  • Method

    The BSK Import Gateways, Simulated

    Nine solved scenarios in a 23-commodity, 7-region IndoTERM model, and what rerouting imports to Bitung, Sorong and Kupang costs.

Research and the SDGs

  • Data page

    SDGs Alignment of Research Strength

    Whether Indonesia's university research strengths line up with the SDG goals its provinces are failing.

  • Data page

    What the Quality Filter Reveals

    978,546 Indonesian journal articles ranked twice, all journals against Q1 only, across 26 subject fields, testing whether publication counts tell you who does good research.

  • Data page

    Single Authorship Across Fields

    28 million journal articles from OpenAlex, 2000–2025. Where most articles still have one author, how fast the share is falling, and code to replicate it.

Health, energy and modelling

  • Project

    Health and Labour in the Energy Transition

    Work package 2.3 of Financing a Just Green Transition. An economy-wide model of Indonesia, extended to represent health status and labour supply.

  • Method

    Health and Labour Module for IndoTERM

    Implementation guide for the module. Three pieces of new work in six stages, each with a procedure, a verification condition and a completion gate.

  • Study guide

    MONASH-Health: A Study Guide

    Verikios, Dixon, Rimmer and Harris (2013) in twenty study steps with self-tests: the labour-supply specification, the HILDA transition matrix, the model core and the results.
